# Book synopses: original, spoiler-light summaries. Header: === number

=== 1
Arthur Dent's house is due to be knocked down for a bypass, and so, it turns out, is the Earth. His friend Ford Prefect, secretly a researcher for the Guide, gets them both off the planet seconds before the Vogons demolish it. After surviving Vogon poetry and an airlock, they are picked up by the stolen starship Heart of Gold, crewed by Zaphod Beeblebrox, Trillian and Marvin. The ship takes them to the legendary planet Magrathea, where Arthur learns what the Earth was really for, and why two mice are very interested in his brain.

=== 2
Pursued by the Vogons, the crew of the Heart of Gold are scattered. Zaphod goes looking for the man who really rules the Universe, survives the Total Perspective Vortex, and dines with the others at Milliways, the restaurant at the end of time. A stolen ship and a series of bad decisions leave Arthur and Ford stranded on prehistoric Earth with a shipload of Golgafrinchan exiles, where they discover what happened to the experiment the Earth was running.

=== 3
Arthur has lived alone in a cave for five years when a passing sofa offers a way home. He and Ford arrive at Lord's just as a ship full of robots steals the Ashes. With Slartibartfast's help, they learn that the robots come from Krikkit, a world sealed away after it tried to destroy the Universe, and that the pieces of the key to its prison are being gathered. Arthur also learns to fly, meets an old enemy, and finds out about his own future.

=== 4
Arthur hitchhikes back to an Earth that should not exist. Everyone remembers something strange, and the dolphins have vanished. He falls in love with Fenchurch, a woman who once understood how everything could be made right, and they set out to discover what happened to the dolphins and what God's Final Message to His Creation says. Ford, meanwhile, is having problems of his own.

=== 5
Ford discovers that the Guide has new owners with plans for a dangerous new edition. Arthur has settled down as a sandwich maker on a quiet planet, until Trillian leaves him with a teenage daughter he did not know he had. The new Guide falls into Random's hands, and every road leads back to Earth, or rather to every version of it.

=== 6
Written by Eoin Colfer with the blessing of Douglas Adams's estate, the sixth book picks up where the fifth left off. Arthur, Ford, Trillian and Random are rescued at the last moment, and the story follows a group of Earth's survivors on the planet Nano as they search for a god to protect them from the Vogons. Zaphod, Wowbagger and Thor all have parts to play.
